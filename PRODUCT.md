# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Trenton-area car owners who want a premium interior/exterior detail without driving to a shop, plus dealerships and consignment sellers who need vehicles showroom-ready.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for Detailing Giron 1 LLC that books quote requests and calls. Success is a visitor understanding this is premium mobile detailing (not a car wash) and contacting the shop.

## Positioning

Mobile detailing that comes to the client, with work on enthusiast cars and dealership/consignment vehicles. The mechanism competitors without mobile service cannot copy: they bring the detail to the driveway, street parking, home, or workplace.

## Operating Context

Static HTML site cloned from a prior local-service template. Quote forms open an SMS draft on mobile and show an on-page success state on desktop. Photography is the client's own work from detailinggiron.com uploads (sourced as a stand-in for Instagram grid shots). NJ county map stays as the service-area interaction.

## Product Principles

1. Prove the work with the client's photography. Never stock cars or fabricated reviews.
2. Lead with mobile and premium positioning, not wash packages.
3. Flag uncertainty in the open rather than guessing counties, coating, hours, or address.
4. Make contact frictionless: phone, SMS quote, no CAPTCHA theater.
5. Keep the NJ map. Tell the truth about what is confirmed (Mercer / Trenton) versus surrounding coverage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
